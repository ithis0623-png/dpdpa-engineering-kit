# Detailed report on a personal data breach

**Our reference:** [ref] · **First intimation sent:** [date and time] · **Aware time (T):** [date and time]

| Heading | What to write |
|---|---|
| (i) Updated and detailed information on the first description | Everything from template (b), corrected and completed |
| (ii) Broad facts on the events, circumstances and reasons leading to the breach | The sequence of events and the root cause, as far as known |
| (iii) Measures implemented or proposed to mitigate risk | What was done, when, and what is planned |
| (iv) Findings about the person who caused the breach | What you know; "none yet" is an answer |
| (v) Remedial measures to prevent recurrence | Control changes, with owners and dates |
| (vi) Report on the intimations given to affected Data Principals | From the incident log: how many people, which channels, when, how many messages failed and were resent |
